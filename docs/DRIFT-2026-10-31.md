## Shape change — the significant finding

About 30 categories kept their filename but **changed shape entirely**: from a named wrapper
(`{ "impressions_history_posts_seen": [...] }`) to the label/value envelope
(`{ "fbid", "label_values", "media", "timestamp" }`). Tier 1 still resolves them by basename,
so nothing is lost, but each one's `requiredKeys` signature no longer matches.

Affected: `posts_viewed`, `videos_watched`, `suggested_profiles_viewed`,
`posts_you're_not_interested_in`, `advertisers_using_your_activity_or_information`,
`blocked_profiles`, `close_friends`, `follow_requests_you've_received`, `hide_story_from`,
`pending_follow_requests`, `recent_follow_requests`, `recently_unfollowed_profiles`,
`removed_suggestions`, `restricted_profiles`, `profile_based_in`, `instagram_friend_map`,
`instagram_profile_information`, `notification_preferences`, `liked_posts`,
`bug_reports`, `saved_collections`, `saved_posts`, `emoji_sliders`, `polls`, `questions`,
`quizzes`, `story_likes`, `story_reaction_sticker_reactions`.

Additive-only changes: `posts` (+`fbid`, `label_values`, `timestamp`), `messages_inbox`
(+`joinable_mode`).

## What this implies (not yet implemented)

1. **A signature is not a property of a category; it is a property of a category *in one
   format*.** ~30 categories now have two measured shapes. The registry's single
   `signature` field cannot express that. The design change is `signatures: Signature[]`
   (match any), with `signatureStillSatisfied` reported per export.
2. The 12 unrecognised files need promotion rows + schemas once promoted (M7 §4 tooling).
3. Media: 6 orphans appeared, and 399 stored vs 376 — more media, none missing.
4. A user who imported the old export and then this one **would see the mismatch warning**:
   `fmt-92b725` ≠ `fmt-e57990`. That is the intended non-blocking behaviour (ADR 0005).
